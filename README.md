# 🚀 Nexora AI – Smart Learning & Productivity Platform

### Developed by Nagaveni B Kittur

Nexora AI is an AI-powered learning and productivity platform designed to help students study smarter, organize their academic activities, and improve their learning experience through AI-driven features.

## ✨ Features

* 🤖 AI-powered learning assistance
* 📝 Automated notes generation
* 📚 Quiz generation for exam preparation
* 📄 PDF reading and learning support
* 🎯 Goal tracking and productivity management
* 💼 Internship assistance
* 🔐 User authentication and database integration

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Java** – Application development
* **HTML5** – Webpage structure
* **CSS3** – Styling and design
* **JavaScript** – Interactive functionality
* **Google Gemini API** – AI-powered features
* **SQLite** – Database management
* **Visual Studio Code** – Development environment

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Nexora AI is to make learning more efficient and accessible by combining artificial intelligence with student-focused productivity tools.

## 💡 Benefits

* Saves time during exam preparation.
* Supports personalized learning.
* Helps students organize their academic goals.
* Makes study materials easier to understand.
* Encourages productive learning habits.
